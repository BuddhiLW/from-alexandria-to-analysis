# Wave C: follow the originals verbatim

Planned 2026-10-05. Start these as the second batch (Apollonius, Descartes-Fermat, Euler, Cauchy) lands.

Each era gets the same shape as the second batch: its own branch `illus-<era>` off `alexandria`. The
spec is illustrate-brief.md, common-brief.md and raster-brief.md, plus this directive:

> Follow the author's work VERBATIM. Walk the original in its own order. State each proposition in the
> words of the public-domain translation, with the original quoted short. Reproduce the author's own
> argument step by step: his construction, his lettering, his sequence of inferences, his numbers and
> tables. Do not substitute a modern re-proof. Emmy proofs and raster numbers are badges beside his
> argument. Prefer depth over breadth. Quote one or two sentences per output (content filter).

## Eras with a notebook already (deepen against the original)

- **galileo**: Discorsi 1638, Crew and de Salvio 1914. Third Day: the theorems on naturally accelerated
  motion (Theorem I, mean speed; Theorem II, odd numbers; inclined planes). Fourth Day: the parabolic
  trajectory and his table of altitudes and amplitudes, recomputed on raster.
- **kepler**: Mysterium 1596 ch. 2 and 14-20 (the nested solids; his table of distances against
  Copernicus). Astronomia nova 1609 (Mars, the area law, ch. 59 the ellipse). Harmonices mundi V ch. 3
  (the third law). Latin, with the series' own rendering.
- **newton-leibniz**: Leibniz, Nova methodus 1684 (his rules for d, his example). Newton, Principia 1687:
  Book I sect. I lemmas 1-11 (first and last ratios), sect. II prop. 1 (area law), sect. III prop. 11
  (ellipse about a focus gives the inverse square). Motte 1729 English.
- **lagrange**: Mecanique analitique 1788 part II sect. IV (the general equations). Theorie des fonctions
  analytiques 1797 (the derivative as the coefficient of the series).
- **riemann-weierstrass**, **dedekind-cantor**: as in the earlier plan, now walking the originals in the
  corpus.
- **euclid**: Books II, V (with eudoxus), VI, and XII.2 in more depth.
- **archimedes**: Measurement of a Circle prop. 3 (his 3 10/71 < pi < 3 1/7 computation, every root
  estimate on raster); Sphere and Cylinder I.

## New eras (corpus present, no notebook yet; each needs a notebook and a timeline entry)

- **ptolemy**: Almagest I.10-11, the chord table (Heiberg Greek, Manitius German), recomputed on raster.
- **copernicus**: De revolutionibus 1543 I.10 (the order of the spheres) and the chord table.
- **oresme**: Latitudines formarum 1486 (the substitute edition). Graphing a uniformly difform quality,
  and the mean-speed rule. Link forward to Galileo's Theorem I: the same pattern.
- **gauss**: Disquisitiones generales circa superficies curvas 1828 (theorema egregium). English by
  Morehead and Hiltebeitel 1902.
